# SOP: Tobacco Counts (Open and Close)

**Who:** All Village Market staff (opening and closing shifts), and Chrissan
**Owner:** Jeff Meakings
**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## Why we count

Tobacco is our highest-value stock and the easiest to lose. Counting every pack at open and close:
- stops us running out of top sellers. Guests leave and don't come back.
- shows right away if packs go missing.

It takes about **10 minutes** per count once you know the wall.

## Where the sheet is

- The **Tobacco Count binder** is in the **drawer under the OLG terminal**.
- One sheet set (4 pages) covers one week, **Monday to Sunday**.
- Items are grouped **RBH, then Imperial, then JTI**. The UPC column shows the last 4 digits of the barcode, so you can match the pack if two names look alike.

## Opening count

1. Do the count **before the first sale** and **before putting away any delivery**.
2. Count **every pack** of each item, on the wall and in back stock.
   - Write the number of **packs**. A full sealed carton of 20s = **10 packs**; a carton of 25s = **8 packs**.
   - Sail singles: count each single.
   - VEEV: count each box of pods.
3. Write it in today's **OPEN** box. **Write 0 if there are none. Never leave a box blank.**
4. Check it against **last night's CLOSE** number. If it doesn't match, **circle both numbers** and tell Chrissan.
5. Initial the **"Counted by"** box for today's OPEN.

## Closing count

1. Do the count **after the last sale**.
2. Count every pack the same way and write it in today's **CLOSE** box.
3. If a count is **at or below the MIN** number, **circle it**. That means we're running low.
4. Initial the **"Counted by"** box for today's CLOSE.
5. Put the binder back in the drawer under the OLG terminal.

## Deliveries

- Do the opening count **first**, then put the tobacco delivery away.
- Initial the **"Delivery put away"** box for that day.
- Give the invoice to the person scanning invoices as usual. Scan it the same day.

## Tips to go fast

- Count in the same order as the sheet, top to bottom.
- Count sealed cartons first (×10 or ×8), then add the loose packs.
- Use a pen, and write numbers clearly: the sheets are read by the system. If you make a mistake, cross it out and write the right number beside it.

## Never

- Never leave a box blank, and never guess a count. Count it.
- Never change a number from an earlier shift. If you think it was wrong, circle it and tell Chrissan.
- Never take a sheet out of the binder, except to scan it on Monday.

## Every Monday: scan last week's sheets

1. Do the Monday opening count on a **new** sheet set, and write the date in "Week starting MONDAY".
2. Take **last week's** pages out of the binder and check the week date is written on them.
3. Scan all pages together as one scan using the **"Invoice"** preset on the Epson scanner (see SOP - Scanning Invoices).
4. Wait for "upload complete".
5. Put the scanned pages in the back of the binder. Chrissan shreds them after 1 week.

## For Chrissan

- **Every Monday**, export the L-BOSS **"PLU by sub-dept"** report for **sub-dept 15 (Tobacco)** for last Monday to Sunday. Save it as a PDF in **Operations > Tobacco Counts > L-BOSS Tobacco Sales**. Without it, the system can't check for missing packs.
- **Tuesday morning** you'll get the **Tobacco count review** email:
  1. **Missing packs:** the count says more was used than L-BOSS says was sold. Check for wrong-button sales (e.g. rung as a different brand), damaged or returned packs, and counting mistakes. If it keeps happening on the same items or shifts, tell Jeff.
  2. **Close did not match next open:** a counting error, or stock moved overnight. Follow up with the people who counted.
  3. **Sheet problems:** missed counts, missing initials or missing pages. Coach the shift.
  4. **Suggested order** for each supplier: order up to PAR, rounded to full cartons. Adjust for holidays and anything already on order.
- **Circled MIN numbers** during the week mean an item is running low. Top it up on the next order, or sooner for top sellers.
- **Changing Min, Par or the item list:** edit the **Items** tab in **VM Tobacco Count Log**, then ask Jeff to have Claude reprint the sheet.
- **Printing:** print **VM Tobacco Count Sheet - PRINT** (Operations > Tobacco Counts) **landscape, letter size**. Keep 4 weeks of blank sets in the binder.
- **Shredding:** shred scanned sheets that are more than 1 week old.
