# SOP: Scanning Invoices to Drive

**Who:** All Village Market staff
**Owner:** Jeff Meakings
**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## Why this matters

Every paper invoice has to be scanned **the same day it arrives**. Once it is scanned, the system enters the bill for Jeff and checks every item cost. If a cost has gone up, Chrissan gets an email that night so she can fix the shelf price.

An invoice left sitting in the office means we sell at the old price, and we lose money on every unit until it is scanned.

## What to scan

- Every supplier invoice or delivery slip with prices on it
- Credit notes, return slips and pick-up slips
- Paper statements from suppliers
- Any bill that comes in the mail (hydro, insurance, etc.)

**Never scan** anything personal or about staff into this folder: no pay stubs, tax forms, IDs or personal papers.

## Step 1: Check the delivery first

1. Count the delivery against the invoice.
2. If anything is short, damaged or wrong, write it on the invoice and have the driver initial it.
3. Tell Chrissan about any shorts or damage.

## Step 2: Get the invoice ready

- Remove staples and paper clips, and unfold the pages.
- **Scan one invoice at a time.** If an invoice has several pages, scan all of its pages together. Do not mix two different invoices in one scan.
- Smooth out crumpled or wet pages. If a page is torn, scan it anyway.

## Step 3: Scan (Epson scanner in the office)

1. Put the pages **face down, top edge first** in the feeder. It holds up to 20 sheets.
2. On the touchscreen, tap the **"Invoice"** preset.
3. Wait for the screen to confirm the upload is finished.
4. Repeat for the next invoice.

The scan goes straight to Google Drive: **1Purchase Invoices VM > 11 VM SCANNED**.

## Step 4: Check it worked

- Open the Google Drive app and go to **11 VM SCANNED**. The new file should be there within a minute or two.
- Open it and check that it is readable and every page is there.
- If it is blurry, cut off or missing pages, scan it again. You do not need to delete the bad copy; the system catches duplicates.

## Step 5: File the paper copy

- Put the paper invoice in the **Scanned** tray in the office. Do not throw it away.

## Do not

- **Do not rename, move or delete** anything in 11 VM SCANNED. The system names and files every invoice itself.
- Do not write prices or notes on invoices for the system. It does not read handwriting. Tell Chrissan instead.
- Do not wait to "save up" invoices for one big scan. Scan the same day.

## If the scanner is not working: use your phone

1. Open the **Google Drive** app. You must be signed in to the store account.
2. Tap **+** and then **Scan**.
3. Take a photo of each page in good light, flat on the counter. Tap **+** to add more pages of the same invoice.
4. Tap **Save**, choose the folder **1Purchase Invoices VM > 11 VM SCANNED**, and save.
5. Tell Chrissan that the scanner is down.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Paper jam | Open the scanner cover, pull the paper out gently, and scan again |
| Two pages went through together | Fan the stack and rescan. Feed thin or curled pages one at a time |
| Scan didn't show up in Drive | Check that the scanner is on the store Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi icon on the screen). Scan again. If it still fails, use your phone |
| The "Invoice" preset is missing | Use the phone method and tell Chrissan |
| Not sure if something should be scanned | Scan it. It is better to scan too much than too little |
