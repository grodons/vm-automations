# SOP: Scanning Invoices

**Who:** All Village Market staff
**Owner:** Jeff Meakings
**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## Why this matters

Every paper invoice has to be scanned **the same day it arrives**. Once it is scanned, the system enters the bill and checks every item cost. If a cost has gone up, Chrissan finds out that night so she can fix the shelf price.

An invoice left sitting in the office means we sell at the old price, and we lose money on every unit until it is scanned.

## What to scan, and what NOT to open

**Scan:** paperwork handed to you with a delivery:
- supplier invoices and delivery slips with prices on them
- credit notes, return slips and pick-up slips

**Do NOT open mail.** Mailed or sealed envelopes go **unopened into the invoice binder** for Jeff to pick up. This includes envelopes from suppliers.

**Never scan** anything personal or about staff: no pay stubs, tax forms, IDs or personal papers.

## Step 1: Check the delivery first

1. Count the delivery against the invoice.
2. If anything is short, damaged or wrong, write it on the invoice and have the driver initial it.
3. Tell Chrissan about any shorts or damage.

## Step 2: Get the invoice ready

- Remove staples and paper clips, and unfold the pages.
- **Scan one invoice at a time.** If an invoice has several pages, scan all of its pages together. Do not mix two different invoices in one scan.
- Smooth out crumpled or wet pages. If a page is torn, scan it anyway.

## Step 3: Scan (Epson scanner in the office)

1. Put the pages **face down, top edge first** in the feeder. It holds up to 20 sheets.
2. On the touchscreen, tap the **"Invoice"** preset.
3. **Wait for the screen to say the upload is complete.** If it shows an error, scan the invoice again.
4. Repeat for the next invoice.

The scanner sends each scan straight to the right place. You do not need to log in to anything.

## Step 4: File the paper copy

1. Write **today's date** on the front of the **Scanned Invoices folder** if it is the first one of the day.
2. Put the scanned paper invoice in the **Scanned Invoices folder**.
3. Paper invoices are kept for **1 week, then shredded**. Chrissan shreds anything more than 1 week old.

## Do not

- Do not open mailed or sealed envelopes. They go in the invoice binder for Jeff.
- Do not write prices or notes on invoices for the system. It does not read handwriting. Tell Chrissan instead.
- Do not wait to "save up" invoices for one big scan. Scan the same day.
- Do not throw away or shred an invoice that has not been scanned.

## If the scanner is not working

1. Put the invoice on **Chrissan's desk** with a sticky note saying "NOT SCANNED".
2. Tell Chrissan that the scanner is down.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Paper jam | Open the scanner cover, pull the paper out gently, and scan again |
| Two pages went through together | Fan the stack and scan again. Feed thin or curled pages one at a time |
| Screen shows an upload error | Check the Wi-Fi icon on the screen and scan again. If it still fails, give the invoice to Chrissan |
| The "Invoice" preset is missing | Give the invoice to Chrissan |
| Not sure if something should be scanned | If it came with a delivery, scan it. If it came in the mail, put it in the invoice binder unopened |

## For Chrissan only

- **Check every day** that the day's scans are in Google Drive: **1Purchase Invoices VM > 11 VM SCANNED**. A file that is blurry, cut off or missing pages needs to be scanned again. You do not need to delete the bad copy; the system catches duplicates.
- **Scanner down:** scan any "NOT SCANNED" invoices with the Google Drive app on your phone. Tap **+** then **Scan**, photograph each page flat in good light, tap **Save**, and choose **11 VM SCANNED**.
- **Shred weekly:** shred paper invoices in the Scanned Invoices folder that are more than 1 week old. Only shred after you have checked they are in 11 VM SCANNED.
- Do not rename, move or delete anything in 11 VM SCANNED. The system names and files every invoice itself.
