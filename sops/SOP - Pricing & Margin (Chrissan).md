# SOP: Pricing & Margin - Keeping Every Price at or Above Target

**Who:** Chrissan (Store Manager, Village Market)
**Owner:** Jeff Meakings
**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## Why this matters

Village Market is a ski-resort store. Our rent and costs are much higher than a store in town, and our guests are paying for convenience: they are on the hill and we are the store that is there. Every item must earn its department's target margin.

When a supplier raises a cost and the shelf price stays the same, we lose money on every unit until the price is fixed. **Your number one pricing job is to reprice cost increases fast.**

## The rule

1. **Every item must be priced at or above its department target margin.**
2. **Every cost increase is repriced by 12 noon the day after the email arrives.**
3. **Tobacco:** you are told about every cost change. Use your judgement on the shelf price.
4. **Lottery:** OLG sets the price. Do not change it.

**Margin** = (shelf price - cost per unit) / shelf price, before tax.
Example: a drink costs $1.50 per bottle. Beverages target is 57%. $1.50 / (1 - 0.57) = $3.49. Price it at **$3.49 or more**.

**No item may ever sell below 35% margin** (except tobacco and lottery). Items under 35% show as **CRITICAL** in your emails and come first.

## What you receive and what to do

### 1. Daily cost-increase email (about 8 pm, only when something went up)

Subject: **"Cost increases to review - X items - [date]"**

Each line shows: the item, the vendor, the old cost and the new cost, today's shelf price, the margin now, the department target, and, if it is now under target, a red line **"BELOW TARGET: price at $X.XX"** or **"CRITICAL: price at $X.XX"**.

**What to do (by 12 noon the next day):**

1. Work from the top. CRITICAL items first, then BELOW TARGET.
2. For each red line, change the retail price in L-BOSS to **the price shown or higher**. Never lower.
3. Print and hang the new shelf tag the same day.
4. Lines with **no red line** are still above target after the cost increase. No change is needed, but you may raise the price if it is clearly cheap for the resort.
5. **Tobacco lines** show "no margin target". Decide using your judgement: what competitors nearby charge, how fast it sells, and whether it is a top seller. Most of our tobacco sits around 35% margin. Keep it there or better where you can.
6. **"Check:" notes** (in italics) mean the line may be a misread: a pack-size change, a jump over 50%, or no item code. Open the invoice link in the email and confirm the cost before you change the price. If it looks wrong, reply to Jeff instead of repricing.
7. "Not matched to a POS item" means the system could not find the item in L-BOSS by its barcode. Find it by description, check its margin yourself using the table above, and fix the price if needed.
8. When the email is done, delete it.

### 2. Monday margin check (about 7 am, only when items are below target)

Subject: **"Margin check - X items below target"**

This is every item in the store that is selling below its department target, lowest margin first (up to 40 per email).

**What to do (by end of day Wednesday):** reprice each item to the "New price" shown or higher, and hang new tags. If the full list is longer than 40, the rest are on the **Margin Check** tab of the VM Price & Margin Log (Operations > 00 Claude > Pricing & Margin).

### 3. Never-Out list (stock, not price)

The **NEVER OUT** tab of "VM Winter Best Sellers" (Operations > 00 Claude > Pricing & Margin) lists the 187 items that made half of last winter's sales. Keep at least the **"Min on hand"** quantity in the store at all times from mid-December to March Break. Tick "Stocked?" as each one is set up. Order extra before Christmas week, New Year's, Family Day and March Break.

## Speed depends on invoices getting scanned

The system can only see a cost increase once the invoice is in the system.

- **Scan every paper invoice on the day it is delivered** into Purchase Invoices > **11 VM SCANNED**. All pages, one invoice per file, readable.
- Emailed invoices are picked up automatically.
- The system reads invoices every evening, so an invoice scanned at 4 pm becomes a price email that same night. An invoice left in the office for three days means three days of selling at the old margin.
- Keep the paper original in the To Shred tray until the next morning. If Jeff's summary says "RESCAN", rescan it from the tray.

## Rules for setting a price

- **Price at the target price or higher. Never lower** without asking Jeff.
- Round up to a price ending in **.49, .79, .99** (or .29 for small items). Never round down below the target price.
- Price **per unit the guest buys** (one bottle, one bar), not per case.
- Keep sizes in line: a bigger size must not cost more per unit than a smaller size of the same item unless that is deliberate.
- If a competitor or guest complaint makes you think a price is too high, tell Jeff. Do not drop it below target yourself.
- Never change a price because of a note written on an invoice or an email from a supplier. Only the system emails and Jeff decide pricing.
